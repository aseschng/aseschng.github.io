Subsection 3.2.2 FYP (Final Year Project) presentation matters

FYP presentation information: FYP presentation requires you to speak (without looking at a script) for about 20~23 minutes with additional 5 minutes of Q and A. The objective of the presentation is for the examiner to understand and appreciate what you have done. Students are expected to speak naturally about their work and engage actively with the examiner. Hence in the presentation you should Present an Overview of the problem – what is the problem, motivation to solve the problem, existing issues of the problem. Discuss Who/how has the problem been solved by others (lit survey). Restrict the discussion to information related to the focus and contributions of your project. Present how you have solved the problem – novelty, difficulty, creativity, etc. Show the results (demo, screen shot, experiment, table, video) of your work. Finally, present conclusions and future works. Some key idea of how you should organize the slides. The main idea is use lots of figures to tell the story, and the figure flows naturally by zooming in and out to see 'where' and what are we talking about without losing context, i.e, we always know where/what are being discussed. An Example below: Watch Marcus video: 2025 Marcus FYP Recorded Presentation and study its style. Some comments below: See that in 0:17~32: he setup the overview of WHAT is his project about Using figure 0:36, he shows a birds eye view of his application (frontend, backend, database) and its relationship to the cloud (ASR service). This is important for the examiner to understand the big picture. The new picture 0:1:26 then zooms out showing the miniaturised application connecting to the cloud deployment for scalability now 0:1:45 zooms in again to see the varius blocks that he has to deal with for the project Have a demo video showcasing what happens - 0:02:09 ~ heavily edit it with highlight text to show whats happening (ensure font not too small, and have boxes to show whats important). Do not just capture the screen, ensure the focus of the examiner is on whats important to take note

Note: For your slides: page 1 MUST have your name, FYP title, year of FYP, etc and all slides must have page number.

Note, the motive of the presentation is for the examiner to grade you, hence he/she has to appreciate your FYP, your work (difficulty, your contribution), and your technical ability, coding skill, knowledge, etc. You should have a lot of good things to say about the great things you have done, but remember you only have 20~25 minutes.

To prepare, I suggest you make a video of your own presentation and watch it as rehearsal. See examples of previous students FYP presentation in the section below.

Subsection 3.2.3 FYP final report and interim report

Pls write your report in Overleaf Latex.

FYP report (interim) and (final): the purpose of the report is to show that you

  1. your project scope (problem) and what you have accomplished.
  2. have done the work by yourself (no cut and paste), able to describe the technology and steps taken to complete the project
  3. have sufficient knowledge of the work (lit review, discussions)
  4. implemented reasonable baselines (codes, demos, etc)
  5. proposed future works

The interim report should look like a 50% version of the final report, and you can look at the examples in the next section. Final report should be in the range of 40~65 pages

See the advise for writing here: WritingThesis, same applies.
